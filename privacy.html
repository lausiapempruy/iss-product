Privacy Policy

This page describes the basic privacy approach for the ISS Product website.

Information collected

The static ISS Product website does not require an account to browse product information. It does not use a custom visitor counter, customer counter, or locally stored fake profile system.

External services

Product buttons may link to external services such as Roblox Creator Store. Those services have their own privacy policies and data practices.

Local storage

The website may use browser local storage for a local display preference such as light or dark appearance. This information is not used as an account or identity system.

Contact

For questions regarding this website, use the contact information provided on the Contact page.
