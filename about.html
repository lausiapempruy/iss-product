ABOUT ISS PRODUCT

Products for Roblox projects.

ISS Product is the product line of Indo Squad Studios, focused on Roblox systems that can be installed, configured, and used inside real experiences.

What ISS Product focuses on

The products are built around practical Roblox Studio workflows. That means configuration is kept visible where useful, interfaces are designed for the devices players actually use, and installation instructions describe the actual Roblox objects involved.

Design approach

ISS Product uses a restrained visual language rather than decorative effects for their own sake. Typography, spacing, contrast, and clear interaction states are prioritized over animated backgrounds, artificial statistics, or promotional UI that does not provide useful information.

Publisher

Indo Squad Studios develops and maintains ISS Product systems for Roblox experiences.
